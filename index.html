01 — Overview

Shelterbound (“the Game”) is a paid top-down survival sandbox game for Android. This Privacy Policy describes how information is handled when you install and play the Game.

The Game is intended to be ad-free and does not require an in-game account.

02 — Information we collect

For the offline version described by this policy: if the Game operates offline and includes no analytics, tracking, advertising, or other data-transmitting SDKs, the developer does not collect or transmit personal information through the Game.

Your device, operating system, Google Play, or services you independently use may process information under their own policies. If future versions add online features or services that change data handling, this policy will be updated.

03 — Local game saves

The Game may store progress and settings locally on your device so you can continue playing. For the offline version, this local save data is used for gameplay and is not sent to the developer by the Game.

Uninstalling the Game or clearing its storage may delete local saves. Device-level backup features, if enabled, are governed by the relevant platform settings and policies.

04 — Purchases

Shelterbound is offered as a paid download through Google Play. Google Play handles the purchase transaction. The developer does not receive your full payment card details through the Game.

For details about Google’s handling of purchase and account information, read Google’s Privacy Policy.

05 — Third-party services

The Game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play and your Android device may process information for app distribution, security, purchases, or operating-system features under their own terms and privacy policies.

If a future version adds analytics, online multiplayer, crash reporting, advertising, or other third-party integrations that change data handling, this policy must be updated accordingly.

06 — Children’s privacy

The Game is not designed to knowingly collect personal information from children. The developer does not intentionally collect personal information through the offline Game as described in this policy.

The age groups selected in the Google Play listing should reflect the Game’s actual content and intended audience.

07 — Security and retention

Local game data is stored on the device using the app’s storage facilities. The offline version described here does not use a developer-operated player account or database.

08 — Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Shelterbound or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this same URL. The “Updated” date at the top will be revised when changes are made.
